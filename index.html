PhD Candidate in Economics · HEC Paris

I am a 6th year PhD candidate in Economics at HEC Paris, under the supervision of Gaetano Gaballo. I will be on the 2026–2027 academic job market.

My research lies at the intersection of macroeconomics and finance. I investigate firms' credit relationships with both bank and non-bank lenders, focusing on the role of information acquisition by lenders and imperfect competition.

I was a visiting student at UPenn in fall 2024, and I took part in the European Central Bank's Graduate Research Programme in summer 2025. My Job Market Paper was awarded the 2026 Lamfalussy Research Fellowship.
